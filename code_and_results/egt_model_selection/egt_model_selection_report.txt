EGT borrower model selection experiment
================================================================================
Design:
- Pure RL base APR + each borrower screening model
- Fixed 6%, 8%, 10% APR + no borrower screening baselines
- Capitals: 1M, 5M, 10M
- Borrower EGT alpha: 0.3
- Borrower EGT eta: 200.0
- Default scenarios: none, low, medium, high

Overall best financier: Fin_RL_Borrower_RL
Overall best borrower model: rl
Overall best total final capital: 262,435,481.13

System run metrics:
- System_Average_Utilization is the average run-level utilization across financiers.
- System_Default_Amount_To_Total_Capital_Rate is total default amount divided by total initial capital across financiers in that run.

Best EGT borrower model: egt_linear_reinforcement
Best EGT financier: Fin_RL_Borrower_EGT_Linear
Best EGT total final capital: 194,638,283.73
